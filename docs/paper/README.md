# The normal-map paper

[`ER_NORMAL_MAPS.md`](ER_NORMAL_MAPS.md) sets out how Elden Ring reads normal maps and tangents, how that was measured,
and the maths behind it: the tangent-bitangent-normal frame, handedness, tangents in the common convention, the ER
Tangents import step, and matching the map to the tangents instead. Every formula is stated in words as well.

It follows the IEEE style (abstract, numbered sections and equations, tables numbered TABLE I, II and so on with
captions above them, numbered references, with dates written day first in UK order rather than IEEE's US order)
as a house style; it is not
submitted anywhere. For the settings to use, see the practical guide, [`../NORMAL_MAPS.md`](../NORMAL_MAPS.md).

The paper names no tool: it describes the importer and Blender's own operations in general terms. It uses standard
mathematical and computer-graphics terms, in British spelling. Fig. 2 is the guide's; Figs. 1, 6, 7 and 11 are
versions of the guide's figures with general labels (`../figures/paper_*.svg`); Figs. 3, 4, 8 and 10 are the paper's own,
drawn by [`../figures/axis_swap_figure.py`](../figures/axis_swap_figure.py) (Fig. 3: (a) animated, (b) static) and
[`../figures/paper_figures.py`](../figures/paper_figures.py). The SVG figures are generated by
[`../figures/make_figures.py`](../figures/make_figures.py). Figs. 5 and 9 are in-game screenshots from the tests, cropped
to the test surfaces, in [`images/`](images/). The formulas are functions in
[`../figures/tangent_geometry.py`](../figures/tangent_geometry.py) and, for the axis swap and views,
[`../figures/axes.py`](../figures/axes.py); the check of the vanilla tangent frame is
[`../figures/frame_checks.py`](../figures/frame_checks.py), run on the 1,515 part models by
[`../figures/frame_survey.py`](../figures/frame_survey.py) (results in `../figures/frame_survey.json`). The layout
test is
[`../figures/integrability.py`](../figures/integrability.py); its results on the game's part normal maps are in
`../figures/integrability_maps.json`, read by [`../figures/layout_results.py`](../figures/layout_results.py).
`tests/test_figures.py` and `tests/test_paper.py` check
them and the paper's worked example.

## Chinese version

[`ER_NORMAL_MAPS.zh-CN.md`](ER_NORMAL_MAPS.zh-CN.md) is the paper in Simplified Chinese, with the same sections,
equations, tables, citations and references (kept in their published form, with the access dates in Chinese). Its
figures are the paper's figures with Chinese text, `../figures/*.zh.svg`, written by `make_figures.py` from the
catalogue in [`../figures/zh_text.py`](../figures/zh_text.py); the English figures are unchanged. The Chinese terms
are fixed in [`glossary.zh-CN.md`](glossary.zh-CN.md). `tests/test_paper_zh.py` checks the Chinese paper against the
English one (equations, table numbers, figures, citations, references and counts), and `tests/test_figures_zh.py` checks
the Chinese figures. A change to the English paper is made in the Chinese one too.

## Reproducing the measurements

The paper cites this repository for the scripts and results of Sections IV-A and IV-B. The committed results
(`../figures/integrability_maps.json` and `../figures/frame_survey.json`) give every number in those sections' tables,
and the tests check the tables against them. Regenerating the results needs your own copy of Elden Ring, whose files
are not distributed here.

1. **Unpack the parts.** Unpack the game's `parts/*.partsbnd.dcx` binders, leaving out the `_l` (low-detail) ones,
   and then the `.tpf` texture files inside them, into one folder, with a BND tool such as WitchyBND.
2. **IV-A, the layout test:** `python docs/figures/integrability.py <unpack folder>` tests every `*_n.dds` file (one
   per file name) and rewrites `integrability_maps.json`. It needs numpy and Pillow.
3. **Build the FLVER dumper** in [`../figures/flverdump/`](../figures/flverdump/), a small .NET 9 program that writes
   each mesh of a FLVER as JSON. It is built against SoulsFormatsNEXT (GPL-3,
   https://github.com/soulsmods/SoulsFormatsNEXT), which is not included here:
   `dotnet build docs/figures/flverdump -c Release -p:SoulsFormatsProject=<path to SoulsFormatsNEXT>/SoulsFormats/SoulsFormats.csproj`.
4. **IV-B, the frame survey:** `python docs/figures/survey_parts.py <path to flverdump.exe> <unpack folder>` dumps and
   surveys every `.flver` (one per file name) and rewrites `frame_survey.json`. It keeps its progress in the unpack
   folder, so a stopped run resumes.

## Exporting to Word or Google Docs

`python docs/paper/export_docx.py` writes `docs/paper/out/ER_NORMAL_MAPS.docx` (git-ignored), with every equation as a
native Word equation, the tables with their captions above them, and the figures as images: still figures as PNGs in
light mode, Figs. 3(a) and 7 as animated GIFs, and the in-game screenshots as PNGs. The pages are US Letter with 0.75 in
margins and their number centred at the bottom; each table's columns are sized to its content, so no word breaks
inside a cell; no figure is taller than 7 in, and each stays on the page of its caption. The line linking the paper in
the other language is left out. It needs pandoc, ffmpeg, Google Chrome and the Python package `websocket-client`, and
reports what the file holds.

`python docs/paper/export_docx.py --lang zh-CN` exports the Chinese paper the same way, to
`docs/paper/out/ER_NORMAL_MAPS.zh-CN.docx`, with its tables captioned 表 I and so on, the bracketed part of 表 I and
表 II's first column on its own line, and its own figures. Chinese text is set in Noto Sans SC, the name Google Docs
offers for Source Han Sans SC, and the figures are drawn in Source Han Sans SC. Add `--serif` for a serif edition,
`ER_NORMAL_MAPS.zh-CN.serif.docx`: body text in Noto Serif SC and Noto Serif, headings, table headers and captions in
Noto Sans SC and Noto Sans. The edits made to the Word file after pandoc are in `docx_tables.py` (tables),
`docx_styles.py` (fonts and styles) and `docx_xml.py` (the rest).

To get a Google Doc, upload the `.docx` to Google Drive and choose **Open with > Google Docs**; Google Docs turns the
Word equations into its own. If the two GIFs do not animate there, insert `paper_axis_swap_animated.gif` (Fig. 3(a))
and `paper_dome_tilts.gif` (Fig. 7) from `docs/paper/out/` in place of the stills (**Insert > Image > Upload from
computer**); for the Chinese file, `paper_axis_swap_animated.zh.gif` and `paper_dome_tilts.zh.gif`.
