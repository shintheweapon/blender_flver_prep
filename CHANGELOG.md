# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Documentation
- `docs/NORMAL_MAPS.md` has figures, generated by `docs/figures/make_figures.py`: the layout of a normal map, the
  tangent frame in perspective, the directions in which the game reads green and red with tangents along +U, and an
  animated figure of what that does to a raised dome, and a figure of the ways out of Blender. The page explains
  `N × T` and the left-hand rule.
- The page and the README name FbxImporter's mirror setting per release: Transform in 1.11.0, a Mirror check box in
  1.7.0 to 1.10.2 (it mirrored X in 1.3 to 1.6.1).
- ER Tangents starts ticked for Elden Ring FLVERs only; the page says so and links the upstream pull request.
- A paper, `docs/paper/ER_NORMAL_MAPS.md`, sets out the convention, how it was measured, the maths with every formula
  explained in words, handedness, the common tangent convention and the game's, ER Tangents and matching the map
  instead, with references; the guide links to it, its section "What FbxImporter writes" is now "Tangents in the
  common convention", and Figs. 3 and 4 title those panels "Tangents along +U". The paper describes the importer and
  Blender's own operations in general terms, with its own versions of Figs. 1 and 3 to 5; the guide keeps the tool
  settings. The paper uses standard mathematical and graphics terms (vector, scalar, component, normalised,
  tangent-space normal). It states the modding community's view that blue holds gloss, untested here, shows how the
  vanilla frame was measured, with the check as a tested function (`docs/figures/frame_checks.py`), and includes
  screenshots of the two in-game tests. It describes ER Tangents as an import step, with or without it, rather than as
  a check box.
- The paper states how handedness shows on screen (with x to the right and y up, z out of the screen is
  right-handed) and has a figure of the axis swap from Blender into FLVER space, drawn by
  `docs/figures/paper_figures.py` from `docs/figures/axes.py`.
- The paper's worked example has a figure: the triangle in UV space and in game, with its partial derivatives and
  tangent frame.
- A figure in the paper shows the channel changes of Section VII at work on a raised dome: sideways or reflected from
  the map as authored, outward after the change; derived, not tested in game.
- The paper's layout test ran on every part normal map of the game (`docs/figures/integrability.py`, results in
  `docs/figures/integrability_maps.json`): Section IV-A gives a table per file-name prefix and a figure, and states the
  correlation as moderate rather than strong. The guide's summary of the test says the same.
- Every test in the paper (III-C, IV-A to IV-D, the validation of ER Tangents) opens with its question, why it
  matters and how the test answers it, and ends with its result and its impact on the implementation. IV-A says what
  it cannot tell: a map with both channels inverted, and how the game reads the maps.

## 1.0.0

### Added
- **Prepare and Export...** and **Export Copy...** buttons in the FLVER Prep tab: write the `<armature>_fs` collection
  to an FBX file as it is (same vertex positions, normals and corner order; identity object transforms), using Blender's
  FBX exporter with Forward Y, Up Z, no Apply Transform and FBX Units Scale. Names keep the `_fs` suffix.
- The report after an export names the Mirror Z setting to use in FbxImporter; Prepare Copy now records its options
  on the collection for this.
- Prepare Copy shows the armature copy In Front, so its bones are drawn over the meshes.
- The extension declares the `files` permission.
- **Y-Up Views** in the FLVER Prep tab: six buttons turn the 3D Viewport to a fixed orthographic view that shows the
  Y-up copies upright, from -Z, +Z, -X, +X, +Y and -Y. The Z views are named **Face** and **Back** after the side of the
  character they show, from the Mirror Z the active object's `_fs` collection was made with.
- **Side Up** (Y-Up or Z-Down) for the Left and Right views, remembered as an add-on preference.
- **Anti-clockwise** and **Clockwise** buttons: turn the current view 90°, as Blender's Shift+Numpad 4 and 6 do.
- **Remove Vertex Colors** button in the FLVER Prep tab: removes every Color Attribute from the selected meshes or the
  active mesh, optionally keeping the render color. Both options are add-on preferences.
- `docs/NORMAL_MAPS.md`: Elden Ring's normal-map layout and tangent convention, and how to get matching tangents
  through FbxImporter.
- The manifest links to the repository (`website`).

### Changed
- The reports and README name FbxImporter 1.11's **Transform** setting (None or Mirror Z) in place of its
  old Mirror check box.
- Named **FLVER Prep** (was FS FBX Tools); extension id `flver_prep`, sidebar tab **FLVER Prep**. Install it in
  place of FS FBX Tools, whose add-on preferences do not carry over.

### Fixed
- Command tooltips show in Chinese (Blender looks them up in the operator context).
- Prepare Copy keeps the originals' collections: each is recreated inside the `_fs` collection as `<collection>_fs`,
  below the collections the armature and all its meshes share. Before, every copy went straight into the `_fs`
  collection. Export includes the copies in the recreated collections, and running Prepare Copy again removes them.

## 0.1.0

### Added
- **Prepare Copy**, in the 3D Viewport sidebar tab "FS FBX": copies the active armature and its meshes into the
  collection `<armature>_fs`, converted into the space The12thAvenger's FbxImporter reads (Y up, metres, identity
  object transforms). Meshes are the armature's children and the meshes whose Armature modifier targets it.
- Meshes are copied in the armature's rest pose with their other modifiers applied; materials are shared; vertex
  groups and bone names are kept; copied objects end in `_fs`.
- **Mirror Z** and **Flip Faces** options, matching FbxImporter's options of the same names, remembered as add-on
  preferences (off by default).
- Running it again on the same armature replaces its previous `_fs` collection.
- Simplified Chinese interface (zh_HANS), using Blender's own words.
